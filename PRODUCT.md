# Product

## Register

product

## Users
iPhone users who copy things all day (links, addresses, colours, screenshots) and lose them the moment they copy something else. They open Clippd for seconds at a time: paste in what they just copied, or find and re-copy something from earlier.

Secondary audience (case study / portfolio surfaces only): hiring managers skimming a junior designer's portfolio for 1–2 minutes.

## Product Purpose
Clippd is the clipboard history iOS doesn't have. iOS blocks apps from reading the clipboard in the background and shows an "Allow Paste" prompt for foreground reads, so Clippd works inside those rules: Apple's system Paste button (one tap, no prompt) and the Share menu. Success: saving a clip takes one tap and finding it again takes a glance.

## Brand Personality
Curious, crafted, calm. Feels like a well-made Apple utility with one small personal touch (an indigo accent and soft glow). Confident and honest: it explains the platform limits instead of hiding them.

## Anti-references
- Dribbble-shot gloss: floating tilted 3D phones, glossy gradients, fake reflections.
- Corporate UX-template case studies: Problem / Persona / Journey map / HMW boilerplate.
- Walls of text.
- Custom UI that fights iOS: custom drop shadows, non-native controls, decorative glass.

## Design Principles
1. Work with the platform, not around it. Native components and Apple's HIG first; the limits are part of the story.
2. The content is the signal. Clips show themselves (thumbnails, colour swatches) instead of being labelled.
3. Show, don't tell. Real screens carry the explanation; text is a caption.
4. Privacy you can see. Every privacy claim maps to something visible in the product.
5. Honest confidence. Tell the true story of the process with conviction; no invented metrics.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, Dynamic Type and VoiceOver in the app, light and dark for every screen, reduced-motion respected.
