# Clippd Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Clippd is a clipboard history app for iPhone. It is built to keep what you copy on your device.

## What Clippd stores

The clips you choose to save (text, links, colours and images) and your settings. They are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. They are not uploaded anywhere, and they are deleted when you delete them in the app or delete the app.

## What Clippd does not do

- It does not read your clipboard on its own. It only receives content when you tap **Paste** in the app or choose **Clippd** in the Share menu.
- It has no account, no sign-in and no server.
- It does not collect analytics, crash data or any personal information.
- It does not track you or share data with advertisers or anyone else.

## Link previews

When link previews are on, Clippd shows a saved link's title and image by contacting that website directly from your iPhone, the same way Safari would. As with any website visit, that website can see your IP address. No information is sent to the developer or any other party. You can turn link previews off at any time in **Settings › Link Previews**.

## Children

Clippd does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
